## Open WebUI menu transition

The native dropdown and popover motion in `app/globals.css` adapts the `flyAndScale` transition from Open WebUI v0.6.5. It uses the same 8px upward travel, 0.95 starting scale, opacity fade, 200ms duration, and cubic-out easing.

Sources:

- [`Dropdown.svelte`](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui/blob/v0.6.5/src/lib/components/common/Dropdown.svelte)
- [`transitions/index.ts`](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui/blob/v0.6.5/src/lib/utils/transitions/index.ts)
